      What is OmniParse AI?
      

      OmniParse AI is an autonomous, browser-based engine that compresses long source code and technical theory into short summaries without losing meaning. It reads your files or pasted text, plans the summary with a team of AI agents, protects the technical constraints and the keywords you care about, and verifies the result before showing it.
      

      How it works
      

      1. Ingest — drop code, PDFs and notes, or paste text.
      2. Plan & extract — agents map the structure and pull out facts, definitions and constraints.
      3. Compress — the summary is written to your chosen length (10–90%), style and language.
      4. Verify — a critic checks length, focus keywords and unverified identifiers; an editor fixes any gap.
      5. Deliver — compare with the original, trace each point to its source, study with flashcards, export.

      Who it is for
      Students revising theory before exams, developers reviewing unfamiliar code, and professionals who need quick executive summaries of long documents.
      

      Privacy
      Everything runs in your browser. Text goes only to the AI provider you choose, with your own key (Groq, Gemini, OpenAI-compatible), or nowhere at all in offline mode. No server, no account, no tracking.
      

      Tech stack
